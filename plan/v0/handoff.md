## In progress

**Task 1.2: align `atlys-crypto` with RFC-0002.** Branch `arsalann/m1-vault-core`.

Plan for this task:

- [x] Argon2id: RFC-0002 accepted range (19456–262144 KiB, t = 2–4, p = 1) in `Argon2Params::validate`; a `V1_CREATE` constant (256 MiB, t = 3, p = 1). Keep `derive_key_unchecked` for benchmarks only.
- [x] Header MAC: HMAC-SHA256 with constant-time verification, framed as `atlys/v1/header || 0x00 || payload` (`atlys_crypto::mac`), checked against the committed vector.
- [x] Envelope fixtures: fixed-nonce password, recovery, and ingest envelopes generated by an independent pure-Python XChaCha20-Poly1305 (self-checked against published test vectors). Rust opens them; no deterministic-nonce production API.
- [x] Remove "provisional until RFC-0002" notes from `atlys-crypto`.
- [ ] Update docs, this file, and the work breakdown; open the PR.
